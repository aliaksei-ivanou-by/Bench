ROLE SUMMARY

Samara Aerospace is seeking a GNC Engineer to help develop the next-generation satellite attitude-control system for its first flight vehicle, Hummingbird. The engineer will work closely with the CTO and an embedded GNC engineer to characterize, simulate, identify, and iteratively improve a novel spacecraft attitude-control and noise-cancellation system.

The role combines high-fidelity dynamics and kinematics simulation, Monte Carlo verification, system identification, momentum-control algorithm development, and active noise cancellation for platform-jitter suppression. The actuator system is synchronized and multi-joint, and the controller must account for structural resonance while allowing jitter suppression and momentum control to coexist.

The request emphasizes curiosity, kindness, collaboration, high standards, a research mindset, learning through data, and comfort with repeated iteration when the initial model or hypothesis is wrong. It explicitly encourages candidates who do not meet every criterion to apply, especially when they bring a relevant background in satellite attitude control, reaction wheels or control moment gyros, multi-joint robotic trajectories, or audio control and noise cancellation.

The exact workplace, employment or contract model, start date, reporting structure beyond the named collaborators, compensation, and work-authorization requirements are not disclosed in the supplied material.

RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Develop a high-fidelity simulation of the spacecraft plant in MuJoCo.
2. Iterate on the spacecraft simulator as the ground testbed and flight model evolve.
3. Apply momentum- and energy-conservation fundamentals so that the simulated physics remain valid.
4. Run Monte Carlo simulations to verify pointing and stability across dispersed initial conditions, plant parameters, and disturbances.
5. Perform system identification and develop algorithms that automate the identification process on orbit.
6. Design feedforward and feedback control for a synchronized multi-joint actuator system used for momentum control.
7. Account for structural resonance and vibration in the actuator and plant dynamics.
8. Design active-noise-cancellation loops for platform-jitter suppression.
9. Ensure that jitter-suppression control can coexist with momentum control.
10. Characterize, simulate, test, and iteratively improve the novel attitude-control system with the CTO and embedded GNC engineer.
11. Use experimental evidence and data to refine models, algorithms, and engineering decisions.
